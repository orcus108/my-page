about

I'm Vedant. I build products for people technology forgot.

AI is getting much smarter in labs, but most people's daily lives barely change. That's the gap I care about: making hard things feel obvious, and bringing real capability to real people — not just power users on tech twitter.

I'm a sophomore at IIT Madras. I think like a product person first — more interested in who something is for and whether it feels right than in the research paper. Right now I'm building Friday, a fully local voice assistant for Mac.

I want to build from India, for markets that Silicon Valley doesn't optimize for.

If you're building something for real people and want to talk, I'd love to hear from you.
